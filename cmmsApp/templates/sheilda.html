Project 1
Fiji

Automation and DC Drive Upgrade

Automation and DC drive modernisation for a rolling mill in Fiji. The obsolete drive and control system was upgraded while retaining the existing motor, achieving stable current sharing, smoother operation, improved diagnostics and reduced downtime.

Products supplied

  • Master–slave DC drive system
  • DC interposing chokes
  • Snubber networks with film capacitors
  • PLC hardware and control components
  • Encoder-based speed feedback system
  • Protection and safety components

Services provided

  • System study and technical engineering
  • Automation and DC drive integration
  • PLC and drive configuration
  • Protection and safety implementation
  • Site support and commissioning

Work executed

  • Upgraded obsolete DC drive and control system
  • Integrated modern automation while retaining the existing motor
  • Achieved stable current sharing and smooth operation
  • Improved reliability, safety and diagnostics
  • Reduced downtime and maintenance effort
